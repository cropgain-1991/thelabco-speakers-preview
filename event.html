About the event

One room for the people putting AI to work

Join industry leaders, innovators and technology experts at The Ballroom, Sydney Central Hotel on 16 October 2026 to explore how artificial intelligence is transforming healthcare, agriculture and global supply chains through practical solutions, collaboration and innovation.

AI has moved out of the research lab and into hospitals, paddocks and warehouses. This event compares what is working, what is not, and what it takes to turn a promising pilot into something an organisation relies on every day.

Themes

Three sectors on one stage

Where is AI already making a measurable difference, and what would help it spread?

Healthcare

From support with diagnosis to the running of a hospital: easing the load on clinicians and widening access to care.

Agriculture

From satellite and sensor data to advice a grower can use: crop monitoring, yield forecasting, water and inputs.

Global supply chains

From forecast to final delivery: anticipating demand, tracing goods and keeping freight visible across borders.
