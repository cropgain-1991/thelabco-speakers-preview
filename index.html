Where we work

Three sectors, one shared problem

Plenty of data, and too little time to act on it.

Health

Clinicians are short on time, not information. AI takes on the reading, sorting and summarising that slows care down.

  • Triage support for scans and referrals
  • Clinical notes and records, summarised
  • Specialist reach for regional patients

Agriculture

A paddock changes by the day. AI turns satellite, weather and sensor data into decisions a grower can act on.

  • Crop stress and disease detection
  • Irrigation and input timing
  • Yield forecasting

Supply chains

Between the farm gate or factory and the customer sit forecasts, bookings and paperwork. AI keeps goods moving and visible.

  • Demand forecasting
  • Shipment visibility and exception alerts
  • Automated documents and coordination
